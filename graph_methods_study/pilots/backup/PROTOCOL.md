# Backup coverage CPU pilot

The objective gives each target its strongest similarity to an acquired annotation plus half its second-best similarity to a distinct annotation. Under independent label failures this is a two-term approximation to the expected similarity to the nearest reliable annotation. Greedy selection reuses the ordinary facility-coverage objective when the second weight is zero. The first 100 picks come from seeded reverse-13NN binary coverage and are shared within each seed, then both arms buy the same number of noisy labels in batches of 100. The backup coefficient is fixed at 0.5 for every condition. See the [fault-tolerant facility location formulation](https://theory.stanford.edu/~kamesh/fault.pdf) and [noise-aware active sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04506).

Both arms use frozen BYOL features, exact RBF weights on a sparse graph containing each row, its 13 nearest neighbors, and neighbors of its five closest neighbors. The demand set is the unlabeled pool at the start of each batch. Acquired-row lists are selected before reading labels. Five seeds vary the common initial batch and synthetic corruption together and every arm shares its seed's noisy annotation realization. Clean and synthetic 20%/50% symmetric and asymmetric conditions, plus CIFAR-100N, use the same frozen 45,000 training and 10,000 held-out test rows as the previous active audit study. Test images have directed outgoing nearest-neighbor edges to the training pool.

The primary diagnostic is the equal-seed, equal-budget graph-voting accuracy difference at 1,600 and 2,000 labels under symmetric 50% noise, where an earlier rank-only training-pool proxy suggested a benefit. The head always uses standard three-hop inductive voting. Cosine 1NN is a secondary outcome. Budgets 400, 800 and 1,200 check the predicted cost of losing first-anchor coverage early. Clean, asymmetric and CIFAR-100N outcomes bound generalization from this pilot.

This uses sparse acquisition with ordinary soft-label-free graph voting. It is a controlled pilot in this geometry and head.
